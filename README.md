### 👋 A little about me

I work on **speech AI that goes beyond transcription** — combining audio and visual information, operating with low latency, and supporting natural spoken interaction.

At **Technische Universität Braunschweig**, advised by **Prof. Tim Fingscheidt**, my research has evolved along a common thread:

<div align="center">

**👁️ Audiovisual perception** &nbsp;→&nbsp; **⚡ Efficient & streaming AI** &nbsp;→&nbsp; **💬 Real-time conversation**

</div>

Along the way, I've authored or co-authored **17 research publications (7 first-author)**, completed **two Applied Scientist internships at Amazon AGI** (Pittsburgh and Aachen), and supervised or co-supervised **15 student thesis projects**.
